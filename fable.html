Fable

Four hidden flowers sleep in the grid. Bloom all four in as few words as you can. Beat the Par!

1. Plant at the edge

Tap a letter with a blue border (grid edge) and trace a word of 3+ letters through neighboring tiles — any direction, diagonals too. Hit Submit.

3. Wake the flowers

When your garden touches a tile next to a flower petal, that petal opens and shows its letter. Open every petal of a flower and it blooms.

4. Beat the par

Every daily garden is checked by a solver: it's always possible to bloom all four flowers at Par using everyday words. Fewer words = better. Under par is legendary.

Tips

• Longer words cover more ground per stroke.
• Aim your words — direction matters more than length sometimes.
• Stuck with no playable words? Undo is free — rewind and take another path.
