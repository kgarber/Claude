How to Play Slither

Build a snake of words on a 10×10 letter grid. Trace as many words as you can and try to span the board edge to edge for big bonus points.

After "SNAKE" is accepted, those cells fade. The head (E) glows. Start your next word from the head, a neighbor, or any used/edge cell. Words use that cell's letter.
  • Move in any of 8 directions (including diagonals)
  • The current word turns green when it's valid
  • Words must be at least 2 letters long

Starting a Word

  • Tap any unused edge cell to start a fresh word
  • Tap any used cell (faded) that has unused neighbors to start from that letter
  • The "head" cell glows brightest — your main launching point

Scoring

  • 2 letters = 1 pt · 3 letters = 2 pts · 4 letters = 4 pts
  • 5 letters = 7 pts · 6 letters = 11 pts · 7 letters = 16 pts
  • 8+ letters = 16 + 6 per extra letter
  • Gold cells (2x): each gold cell in a word doubles that word's score
  • Chain bonus: 3 words of 5+ letters in a row gives x1.5 on the next word
  • Coverage bonus: +1 point per 5 cells used at game end
  • Cross-board bonus: if your connected snake reaches both opposite edges (left→right or top→bottom), you get bonus points equal to the snake's size

Goals

  • Par 80: a solid game
  • Great 150: excellent play
  • Genius 250: mastery

Tips

  • Use the L→R and T→B bars to track edge-to-edge progress
  • Tap "Undo" to reverse your last word if you released early
  • "New Grid" gives a fresh letter layout if you're stuck
  • The game ends automatically if no valid moves remain
