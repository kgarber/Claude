4. Score More

Longer words = way more points
2 letters = 4 pts · 3 = 9 · 4 = 16 · 5 = 25 · 6 = 36
Use more cells = higher multiplier
Every cell you use adds +0.01x
◆ Diamond cells = big boost
Each diamond adds +0.5x to your multiplier
Bonus words = extra multiplier
4 words shown above the grid. Find one = +0.5x

Example

Say you played these words:

STONE (5) = 5×5 = 25
TRAP (4) = 4×4 = 16
RANK (4) = 4×4 = 16
HER (3) = 3×3 = 9
Word pts = 66

16 cells used, 2 ◆ diamonds, 1 bonus word found:

66 × 1.16 × 2.0 × 1.5 = 229
words × grid (16 cells) × ◆ (2 diamonds) × bonus (1 found)
