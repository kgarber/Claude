Elfer Raus!

The classic "Eleven Out!" card game — now online, now with 100% more dad jokes.

How to play (30-second version)
  • 80 cards: numbers 1–20 in four colors.
  • The red 11 opens the game. Every other 11 opens its color's row.
  • On your turn, play as many cards as you like — each must extend a row by exactly one (12, 13… up / 10, 9… down) or be an 11.
  • Can't play (or don't want to)? Draw 3 cards instead.
  • First player with an empty hand wins. Everyone else gets to say "good game" through gritted teeth.

2–6 players · peer-to-peer, no accounts, no servers, no vegetables
