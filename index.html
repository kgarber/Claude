How to Play

Build a chain of words from START to END.

Rules

Each word must connect to the previous word by one of these links:

  • ABC Letter Link — change, add, or remove 1-2 letters
    cat → bat, cart → car
  • ~ Sound Link — words share a beginning or ending
    night → light, fire → fish
  • ≈ Meaning Link — words are semantically related
    ocean → wave, king → crown

Distance

Each word shows how semantically close it is to the END word:

HOT WARM TEPID COOL COLD

Goal

Reach the END word in as few moves as possible. Par is the target number of moves. A new puzzle appears every day!

Sharing

After solving, share your chain as a color-coded emoji grid — your path tells a story!
